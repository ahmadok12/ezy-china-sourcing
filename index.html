Defect Prevention

Quality Checked Before It Leaves China.

We inspect your goods before shipment to identify quantity issues, visible defects, packaging problems and deviations from agreed specifications.

AQL 2.5 Inspection Standard Explained

We apply ISO 2859-1 (AQL 2.5 Level II) sampling tables to inspect finished product lots: zero tolerance for Critical defects (safety/functional failures), ≤2.5% for Major cosmetic flaws, and ≤4.0% for Minor imperfections.

Air Express

Fastest option for smaller shipments, urgent product samples, and initial validation runs.

• Typical Transit: 3–5 Business Days

• Best For: <100 kg or high-urgency stock

DHL, FedEx, UPS Courier Routing
🛫

Air Cargo

Suitable for shipments where speed matters but volume is larger than typical express parcels.

• Typical Transit: 7–12 Days

• Best For: 100 kg – 1,000 kg shipments

Airport-to-Airport / DDP To Door
🚢

Sea LCL

For smaller commercial shipments that don't require a full container (Less than Container Load).

• Typical Transit: 20–35 Days

• Best For: 1 CBM – 15 CBM shipments

Palletized consolidation from Ningbo/Shenzhen
📦

Sea FCL

For larger volumes requiring a full container (20GP, 40GP, or 40HQ) with dedicated sealing.

• Lowest freight cost per unit

• Best For: Volume wholesale & retail replenishment
