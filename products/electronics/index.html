Rigorous Hardware Quality Control

Electronics Quality Inspection Checklist

1. Electrical & Safety Checks
  • • High-Voltage (Hi-Pot) insulation test
  • • Ground continuity test
  • • Battery overcharge & thermal runaway test
  • • Standby & full-load power consumption
2. Function & Firmware
  • • Bluetooth pairing range & stability (10m)
  • • Firmware version & OTA update validation
  • • Touch button responsiveness (100 cycles)
  • • Indicator LED color & brightness uniformity
3. Mechanical & Cosmetic
  • • USB port insertion/extraction force
  • • Plastic mold parting lines & burrs
  • • Screen scratch and dead pixel inspection
  • • 3M tape adhesion on rubber feet & pads
